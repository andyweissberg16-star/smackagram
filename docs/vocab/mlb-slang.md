# MLB slang and Smacky vocabulary

Source lists supplied by Andrew, 2 Aug 2026. Currently wired into
**Locked & Loaded only** (gated to sport == "mlb" in
trash_talk_service.MLB_SLANG). Kept here in full so the other generators -
the Daily Smack, Smackagram sends, Smack Battle - can draw on the same
vocabulary when they are worked on.

Nothing here is a licence to invent a situation. The situational terms at
the bottom fire only when the box score supports them.

## Catchphrases — one per call at most

- "That baseball just filed for workers' compensation."
- "He hit that thing into next Tuesday."
- "Buddy just struck out so hard the catcher started feeling bad."
- "That swing had absolutely zero parental supervision."
- "That glove's got a restraining order against baseballs."
- "I've seen folding chairs make more contact."
- "That pitch had his ankles filling out change-of-address forms."
- "He's collecting strikeouts like they're Pokemon cards."
- "That bat should file a missing person report for the baseball."
- "Somebody check if his bat is connected to Bluetooth."
- "That home run just raised property values."
- "The only thing he barreled today was disappointment."
- "That swing was sponsored by wishful thinking."
- "He just turned that baseball into airport luggage."
- "That pitcher is serving frozen pizza - every meatball right down the middle."

Note: the home-run ones suit the Daily Smack, where winners get covered.
They do not fit a Locked & Loaded call, which only roasts a loss.

(a zero) · bush league (classless) · frame (half inning) · stranded (left on

## Situational — ONLY when the box score supports it

| Term | Requires |
|---|---|
| Golden sombrero | EXACTLY four strikeouts by one batter |
| Hat trick | Exactly three strikeouts by one batter |
| Mendoza line | A batting average around .200 |
| No-hitter / no-no | Actually held hitless |
| Blown save | A reliever genuinely gave away a lead |
| Warning track power | A fly ball that died short of the fence |

Never invent the situation to fit the term.
