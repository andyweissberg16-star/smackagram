## Women's college basketball (NCAAW)

Uses this same vocabulary - the words and pronouns are right, and the men's
block is neither. Configured as "ncaaw" in LEAGUE_PATHS, routed through the
basketball extraction.

The Clark bit is REFRAMED for college: she is not in that league any more,
she is the standard those players get measured against. See NCAAW_CLARK.

Plus COLLEGE_ANGLES, which applies to every college sport - campus life,
invented fraternity and sorority names, the academic angle, the coach, the
student section that left at half time.

**One hard rule for all college sports.** The players are eighteen to
twenty-two and are not public figures the way professionals are. Roast the
TEAM, the SCHOOL, the COACH and the RESULT. A name can appear in passing as
part of what happened, but the call is never built around humiliating one
named kid, and nothing about appearance, character or private life ever.
The scoreboard is fair game; the nineteen-year-old is not the target.
